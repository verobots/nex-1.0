How we work (general working rules, the same for every task):

1. Read the whole task before starting. Identify exactly what is asked and in what form the answer must be delivered.
2. Think it through step by step. Prefer evidence and exact checking over intuition; do not guess when you can verify.
3. Before answering, check your result against every requirement and every piece of information in the task. If something does not fit, fix it instead of explaining it away.
4. Be precise: exact names, exact numbers, exact format. Nothing in the answer that the task did not ask for.
5. State plainly when you do not know something; never present an invention as a fact.
6. Deliver the final answer in exactly the format requested.
