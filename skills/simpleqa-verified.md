Skill: SimpleQA Verified (short factual questions, no tools)

Source: written only from the public description of SimpleQA and SimpleQA Verified (OpenAI 2024; Google DeepMind 2025) and its public grading rules. No question or answer from the measured items was used.

What this test is
- One short factual question with a single, verifiable correct answer. Answer from memory only; no search.
- A grader compares your answer with a gold answer and gives: CORRECT (contains the gold fact and nothing contradicting it), INCORRECT (any contradicting fact, including a wrong guess among several, or a hedged wrong answer), or NOT_ATTEMPTED (no answer, "I don't know").
- The score rewards both being right and not being wrong: a wrong answer is worse than "I don't know". Listing several candidates counts as wrong.

How to answer
1. Read the question precisely: which entity, which date or year, which unit, which edition or episode, first or last, full name or surname. Many questions hinge on one qualifier.
2. Recall the fact and cross-check it with what else you know (dates consistent with the person's life, numbers in plausible range, the right country or organisation). Questions are often about obscure details; be careful with similar-sounding names and with confusing the year of an event with the year it was reported.
3. For numbers and dates, give the exact value at the precision asked (day, month and year if asked for a date). Do not round unless the question does.
4. Give exactly one answer, stated directly in the first sentence. Do not add alternatives or extra facts that could contradict the gold answer.
5. If after careful recall you are more likely wrong than right, answer "I don't know" instead of guessing.
